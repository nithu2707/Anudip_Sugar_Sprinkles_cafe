Our Legacy

The Story of CFE Restaurant

A journey celebrating royal culinary legacies, artisanal sourdough wood-fired baking, and masterfully blended spices since 2012.

Culinary Heritage

Rooted in Tradition, Elevated by Modern Artistry

CFE Restaurant was born from a singular vision: to create a sanctuary where the profound richness of authentic Indian recipes meets international culinary precision. What started as an intimate café has evolved into an iconic culinary destination.

We believe food is not merely sustenance, but an emotional dialogue between the master chef and the honored guest. Every morning, our chefs source fresh organic vegetables, cold-pressed oils, hand-churned white butter, and farm-fresh poultry to craft each dish from scratch.

"We never compromise on the purity of ingredients or the patience required for genuine slow cooking."

— Chef Vikram Rathore, Culinary Director
Our Guiding Pillars

The CFE Food Philosophy

Every dish served across our 8 categories adheres to strict culinary standards.

🌿

Stone-Ground Aromatics

Spices are dry-roasted and stone-ground in our prep kitchens daily to unlock deep essential aromas without burning delicate oils.

🔥

Wood-Fired Hearth

Our hand-built wood oven burns cured oak timber at 450°C, producing blistered, airy sourdough crusts with irresistible smokiness.

🏺

Dum Pukht Sealing

Biryanis and slow meats are sealed with whole wheat dough rims, allowing food to gently cook in its own juices and steam.

☕

Artisanal Roastery

Single-estate Arabica beans from the misty slopes of Chikmagalur, roasted to medium profile and brewed with clear clear-ice precision.
