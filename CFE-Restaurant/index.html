About CFE Restaurant

A Symphony of Heritage & Contemporary Flavors

Founded with an unyielding passion for gastronomy, CFE Restaurant offers an immersive dining experience where timeless recipes are brought to life through pristine technique and warm Indian hospitality.

Farm-To-Table Commitment

Handcrafted With Pure Ingredients

Every spice is stone-ground in small batches to preserve volatile essential oils. Our slow-fermented pizza dough proofs naturally for 48 hours, while our dum biryanis simmer over glowing wood embers in dough-sealed clay pots.

From organic cold-pressed oils to heirloom grains and single-origin Arabica roasts, we ensure each plate delivered to your table is vibrant, healthy, and unforgettable.
